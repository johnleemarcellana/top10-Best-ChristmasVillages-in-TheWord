1. COLMAR — ALSACE, FRANCE
==========================

Colmar is a charming town in the Alsace region of France. Its old town is known for colorful historic buildings, narrow streets, canals, and beautiful architecture.

CHRISTMAS HIGHLIGHTS
- Several traditional Christmas markets around the historic center.
- Handmade ornaments, gifts, and seasonal Alsatian food.
- Decorated streets around Little Venice and the old town squares.

WHY VISIT?
Colmar is a wonderful choice for travelers who want a traditional European Christmas atmosphere, beautiful architecture, festive shopping, and romantic evening walks.
